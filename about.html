Welcome to ICCT Colleges Tour

This website is a group project made by first-year BSIT students to showcase ICCT Colleges and provide visitors with a simple, organized virtual tour.

About This Project

This website was created as part of CC02 to demonstrate what we’ve learned in coding and web development. Our goal is to use our skills to the best of our ability, improve through this project, and present an easy and engaging virtual tour of our school.

  • Showcase the spirit of ICCT Colleges
  • Present school information clearly and creatively
  • Make it simple for students and visitors to explore

Our Mission

To provide an informative and engaging online tour that helps students and visitors understand ICCT Colleges through a clear and well-organized experience.

Our Vision

To inspire future learners by showcasing the campus, programs, and community of ICCT Colleges in a way that is accessible, modern, and easy to navigate.
